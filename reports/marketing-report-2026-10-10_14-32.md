## 4) Key insights (3–5)
- Email and Organic are the strongest drivers of profitability: Email ROAS 56.68 (spend 434 PLN) and Organic ROAS 35.68 (spend 378 PLN) deliver outsized revenue vs small spend — clear scale candidates.
- Meta retargeting performs very well (Retargeting & Retargeting B: ROAS ~21, low CPAs ~5.3) — retargeting on Meta is efficient and should be scaled before prospecting.
- Significant wasted or low-efficiency spend sits in: LinkedIn (high CPC/CPA, ROAS 4.68), Google Display prospecting (ROAS 1.46), YouTube awareness (ROAS 2.09) and one Partnerships campaign (Affiliate - Coupon ROAS 3.77). These sum to non-trivial spend with poor returns.
- Google Search product/brand search campaigns show acceptable performance (Product A ROAS 13.34, Brand ROAS 10.9) — search intent works; prospecting display/video does not convert well.
- TikTok shows reasonable CPC but low conversion rate (4.03%) — works for awareness/testing but not yet efficient for direct conversions.

## 5) Recommendations — concrete budget reallocation & optimisation

Immediate budget moves (reallocate ~2,509 PLN total):
- Pause / reallocate low-ROAS line items (immediate savings ≈ 2,509.28 PLN):
  - Pause Google Display - Prospecting: save 480.94 PLN (stop until creative/targeting revised).
  - Pause LinkedIn - Traffic: save 933.12 PLN (reassign; LinkedIn continuing only for high-quality lead-gen if needed).
  - Reduce LinkedIn - Lead Gen by 50%: save ≈ 313.68 PLN (keep test but reduce spend).
  - Pause Affiliate - Coupon: save 504.22 PLN (poor ROAS; renegotiate or disable).
  - Reduce TikTok spend by 50%: save ≈ 277.32 PLN (run smaller creative/test bucket).
- Reinvest saved 2,509.28 PLN into high-ROAS channels:
  - Email (+1,000 PLN): expand promo/onboarding/webinar sends and list segmentation A/B tests.
  - Organic / SEO content (+600 PLN): accelerate top-performing pages (comparison, blog CTA) and CRO tests.
  - Meta retargeting & lookalike (+600 PLN): scale retargeting creatives (Meta Retargeting A/B shows high ROAS).
  - Google Search - Product A / Seasonal (+309.28 PLN): increase bids on converting keywords and add negatives for low performers.

Campaign-level optimisation actions
- Google Display & YouTube: pause prospecting creatives, review audiences, apply frequency caps, and A/B test new creatives targeted to high-intent segments; re-enable only after CPA < PL Z 20 or ROAS > 5.
- LinkedIn: move spend to targeted account-based/lead-gen forms only if CPL justified; otherwise reallocate to Meta/Email where conversion efficiency is higher.
- Meta: shift budget from broad prospecting to retargeting, new-creative A/B tests and lookalike audiences; increase bid aggressiveness on top-performing creatives.
- TikTok: concentrate on best-performing creative (Test 2 > Test 1), run landing page experiments to lift conversion rate.
- Partnerships/Affiliate: keep Partner Webinar (good ROAS) but pause underperforming affiliate coupon until terms improve; renegotiate CPA-based commission on coupon placements.

Measurement & thresholds (monitor weekly)
- Channel CPA targets: Email < PLN 2.0, Organic < PLN 4.0, Meta < PLN 12.0, Google Search < PLN 12–15. Pause/adjust channels above 2× target CPA for 2 consecutive weeks.
- Monitor ROAS by campaign weekly; if ROAS < 3 and CPA > 3× channel target, pause campaign.
- Run creative A/B tests (minimum 2-week runs) before scaling new creatives.
